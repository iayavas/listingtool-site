Privacy notice

What this notice covers

This notice covers listingtool, provided by Sevil Turan LLC, and this informational website. Contact sevilturan@outlook.com about privacy or support. Marketplace connections are limited to the private invitation-only Personal Access beta; public downloads and self-service enrollment are unavailable.

Your creative files

Artwork, mockup templates, generated text, submission snapshots, and settings are stored on your computer. Image compositing happens locally. Optional AI features send the artwork, product information, and conversation text you select to the AI provider under your account. If you enable automatic drafts with optional AI writing, this also includes new artwork in the category folders you select and the category's creative settings, without a separate provider prompt for each file. “Local compositing” does not mean that all your data stays on your computer.

Anthropic is used for optional listing writing and Claude-based design assistance. OpenAI is used when you select the Codex-based design provider. These providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not use marketplace content for these creative requests. Research tools are disabled.

Optional file import

If you connect Google Drive, the app reads the files or category folders you select and downloads matching artwork to your computer. With daily automation enabled, it checks those folders for new designs at the configured time. It does not modify or delete Drive files. Local or synced folders can be scanned without connecting a Drive account.

Private beta connection

The invitation-only Personal Access beta uses a per-device session and a server on Fly.io. The server encrypts marketplace sign-in grants in its database; the desktop stores its own session using operating-system encryption. Listing text and media pass through the server when you submit a reviewed snapshot or enable automatic draft creation for your chosen files and category settings. They are not retained as server content.

The server retains connection identifiers, authorization attempts, and submission metadata, including operation and listing identifiers, to prevent duplicate submissions. This is distinct from creative files stored locally. Automatic publication is disabled. Public downloads and self-service connection are unavailable; the owner's live automatic-draft verification is pending before further beta distribution.

Controls and retention

You can stop optional AI use, disable future automatic runs, disconnect services, cancel queued work, and remove local files. Stopping future runs does not remove drafts already created or undo a submission in progress. Disconnecting a supported connector deletes its stored grant and pending authorizations and prevents queued work from using that connection. Revocation at an external service takes effect there; the app clears the unusable connection when detected. We do not claim that external revocation instantly notifies our server.

Local creative files remain until you delete them. Operational logs contain status and counts, exclude listing payloads and credentials, and expire after seven days when the app or server next runs. Submission identifiers are retained while needed to reconcile an operation; deletion requests are handled through support. Support correspondence is retained for the support case and deleted on request unless a legal obligation requires retention.

Marketplace cache behavior

Cached connection identity expires after one minute. Template reads expire after one minute, and shop-setup displays are cleared when the connection or local account changes. Expired displays are refreshed or withheld. Local submission snapshots remain fixed so later cache refreshes cannot silently change their content. Manual approval is attached to its exact snapshot; automatic drafts use the category settings saved for that run. Snapshots are not live displays of marketplace listings.

Website and support

This site loads its own styling without third-party fonts or analytics. Our hosting provider receives normal connection information such as IP address and request time. If you email support, we receive the address and content you send. Do not include passwords, API credentials, or customer data.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information. Contact us to exercise those rights. This business tool is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

We will update this notice before changing the described data handling. The application records acceptance of the version of its terms and privacy notice shown to you. Optional providers and external services have their own notices.
