Draft creation and automation

In the manual workflow, you review and approve a final draft snapshot before submission. Editing that snapshot requires fresh approval. When you enable automatic drafts, the app instead uses your saved category settings and new seller-owned files from your selected folder or Google Drive source to prepare and submit unpublished drafts without a separate listingtool review for each item. Review those drafts in Etsy and decide there whether to publish them. Automatic publication is disabled.

Daily design automation requires a connected shop, an awake computer, and a running app. It runs at your configured time and timezone; missed days are not backfilled. Scheduling individual reviewed snapshots is a separate capability and is currently unavailable. Uncertain submission outcomes require reconciliation and are not automatically replaced. Canceling an operation already in progress cannot undo a draft that the external service may have created.
